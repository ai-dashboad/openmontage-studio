How it works
From a sentence to a finished video.

No timeline wizardry, no After Effects. Three steps.

01

Describe it

Type a brief — “a 30-second explainer on black holes.” A local AI model drafts a scene-by-scene plan.

02

Edit & approve

Tweak headlines, charts, images, timing, and voice. Ask the agent to revise. Approve each stage when it’s right.

03

Render & ship

Export a real 1080p MP4 with cinematic motion graphics and narration. Save it, post it, done.
