# Stage: idea

Turn a one-line request into a clear concept the rest of the pipeline can build on
— the `brief` artifact.

## Quality bar

- **One sentence of intent.** What is this video for, and who is it for?
- **A hook.** The single most interesting angle, stated plainly.
- **Target length and platform.** Default to a ~20–30s short unless told otherwise.
- **Tone.** Pick one (e.g. punchy, calm-explainer, cinematic) and commit.

If the request is ambiguous, ask one sharp clarifying question rather than guessing
on something that changes the whole video. Otherwise, propose and move on — don't
stall a small video with a long interview.
