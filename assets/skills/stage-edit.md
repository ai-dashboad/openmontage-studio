# Stage: edit

Decide how the scenes are sequenced and finished — the `edit_decisions` artifact.

## Quality bar

- **Order and pacing.** Confirm the scene order and that each duration fits its
  narration; a card should never cut before its line finishes.
- **Narration alignment.** Each scene's audio plays within its window; tighten
  durations so there's no dead air and no clipped words.
- **Captions and music.** Decide whether captions burn in and whether a music bed
  plays under the narration (and at what volume, ducked under speech).
- **Render runtime.** Lock how the video is composed and carry it through
  unchanged; don't silently switch the look at compose time.

State the decisions plainly so compose can execute them without guessing.
