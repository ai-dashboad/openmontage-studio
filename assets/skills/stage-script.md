# Stage: script

Turn the concept into the words that will be spoken — the `script` artifact.

## Quality bar

- **Write for the ear, not the page.** Short sentences. One thought at a time.
- **Front-load the hook.** Earn the first three seconds; people leave fast.
- **Cut filler.** If a line doesn't add information or momentum, remove it.
- **Match the length.** A ~20–30s short is roughly 50–80 spoken words. Count.
- **End on a point**, not a trailing "...and that's it."

Keep the structure visible: a hook line, a few body lines, a closing line. This
maps cleanly onto the scene plan that comes next.
